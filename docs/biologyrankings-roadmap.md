**Status:** Phase 1 & 2 Live | Phase 3 Deferred

## 1. Overview & Phased Implementation

BiologyRankings (hosted at `https://ecerankings.org/biology/`, with optional standalone domain `biologyrankings.eding.dev` pending DNS routing) provides an institutional ranking of biosciences and biomedical programs based on fractional author credit in selective peer-reviewed journals, mirroring `ecerankings.org` and `csrankings.org`.

| Phase | Scope | Status | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Phase 1** | **Elite Multidisciplinary Core (4 Venues)** | **LIVE** | *Cell*, *Nature*, *Science*, *PNAS* (2016–2025). Botanical emerald palette. |
| **Phase 2** | **Core Life Sciences & Bioengineering (32 Venues)** | **LIVE** | Field-defining disciplinary anchors across 11 subfields + elite core (149,162 works across 36 total venues, 134,412 primary research articles after front-matter filtering). Full institutional rollups active. |
| **Phase 3** | **Expanded Clinical & Specialty Catalog (50+ Venues)** | **DEFERRED** | Full JAMA, Lancet, NEJM clinical suites, public health series, and secondary open-access cascades. Deferred to prevent hospital clinical trial skew and preserve basic life sciences parity. |

## 4. Permanently Disqualified / Excluded Venues

These venues were formally audited by the bibliometric review and **permanently excluded** from empirical rankings:

| Disqualified Venue | Reason for Permanent Exclusion |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`Nature Reviews (all titles)`** | **Review-Only:** Publishes 100% invited/commissioned literature summaries; produces zero primary bench discovery papers. |
| **`Trends in (all titles)`** | **Review-Only:** Editorial review syntheses, not original research. |
| **`Annual Reviews (all titles)`** | **Review-Only:** Invited annual review series. |
| **`Current Opinion in (all titles)`**| **Review-Only:** Commissioned topic overviews. |
| **`NEJM Journal Watch`** | **Digest/Summaries:** Curated executive summaries of outside trials; no original research. |
| **`PLOS ONE`** | **Validity-only Mega-Journal:** Does not select for novelty or biological significance. Including 15,000+ unselective papers/yr would turn rankings into a grant-volume proxy. |
| **`eLife` (Post-2023)** | **No Rejection Gatekeeping:** Abandoned traditional peer-review accept/reject thresholds in 2023 in favor of uncurated reviewed preprints. |
| **`Nature Chemical Engineering`** | **Non-Biological:** Industrial catalysis, transport phenomena, and petroleum/polymer chemical processes. |
| **`PLOS Climate` / `Water` / `Sustainability`** | **Non-Biological:** Civil hydrology, climatology, and public policy. |
| **`The Lancet Planetary Health`** | **Policy / Climate Health:** Environmental epidemiology and climate policy; distinct from biological life sciences. |
| **`PLOS Complex Systems`** | **Unproven / Non-Biological:** Generalized physical/complex network systems with negligible standing in computational biology. |

## 5. Architectural Policies Confirmed

1. **Geometric Mean Scoring:** Just like `ecerankings.org`, institutional scores are the geometric mean of `(1 + adj_count)` across selected subfields minus one, resisting single-area dominance.
2. **Author Attribution:** Fractional author credit (`1/N`) at time of publication, split evenly among co-authors.
3. **Academic vs. All Centers Toggle:**
   * **Academic Only (`type=education`):** Ranks universities without research hospitals/commercial labs.
   * **All Research Centers:** Accords full institutional credit to independent discovery institutes (HHMI, Salk, Broad, Whitehead, MSKCC, Max Planck).
4. **Article-Level Type Filtering:** `type == "article"` is strictly enforced across all OpenAlex queries to eliminate errata, book reviews, editorials, and commentary.

## 6. Pre-Harvest Data Integrity Remediation (Claude Fable 5.2 Audit)

Before executing Phase 2 harvesting, three critical data issues identified during Fable 5.2's audit of `cache_biosciences/` (56,396 works) must be resolved:

1. **Front-Matter / Editorial Credit Leak:**
   - *Problem:* In OpenAlex, `type: article` includes *Nature* News & Views, Previews, Podcasts, and Comment items. In our *Nature* cache, 6,179 works carry the `10.1038/d41586` DOI prefix (editorial/news) compared to 6,409 real research articles (`s41586` or legacy `nature`). Works with $\le 2$ authors carried 25% of all credit in *Nature* and 20% in *Cell*.
   - *Fix:* Enforce DOI/section regex filters: exclude `10.1038/d41586*` for Nature, exclude Cell Previews/Commentary (`< 3 pages`), and require page ranges $> 3$ for *Science* perspectives.
2. **Institutional Rollup & Healthcare Disambiguation:**
   - *Problem:* OpenAlex `type == "education"` excludes UT Southwestern (`healthcare`, ranked #12) while retaining UCSF (`education`, ranked #4). Furthermore, Harvard's credit is split across MGH, Brigham, and Dana-Farber (~380 credits), while HHMI absorbs 552 credits from host universities.
   - *Fix:* Replace the raw OpenAlex `type == "education"` filter with an explicit institutional rollup dictionary (mapping MGH/BWH to Harvard or providing a clear Medical Center unified view).
3. **100-Author List Truncation:**
   - *Problem:* In OpenAlex list endpoints, works with $> 100$ co-authors are capped at 100 authors. While individual works endpoints return the full list, $N$ was capped for 335 mega-author genomics papers.
   - *Fix:* Run a post-harvest check re-fetching full bylines for any work with `len(authorships) == 100`.
