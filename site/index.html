About ECERankings

ECERankings.org is a metrics-based ranking of Electrical & Computer Engineering programs, modeled on CSRankings but built for how ECE actually publishes — journal-heavy, IEEE-centric. It uses OpenAlex as the primary data source with Crossref fallback for conference years missing from OpenAlex.

Methodology

  • Adjusted counts: each paper is worth 1.0, split evenly across its N authors (1/N per author). Institution credit = sum over its authors’ shares.
  • Geometric mean: institution score = geometric mean of (1 + adjusted count) across selected areas — rewards breadth across areas, resists single-area dominance.
  • Selective venues only: only publications in the curated venue list count. A venue joins an area only if it passes both selectivity and scope tests.
  • Education-only: only authors with education-type affiliations (universities) are credited. Industry labs, government agencies, and nonprofit research institutes are excluded.
  • At Time of Publication: each paper is credited to the institution(s) listed on that specific paper, not to an author’s current institution.
  • No citation counts are used — publication counts at selective venues resist gaming.

FAQ

Why not use DBLP like CSRankings?
DBLP is CS-centric and has weak coverage of core EE journals (power, devices, EM, photonics). OpenAlex covers all IEEE/Optica venues with author→institution affiliations per paper (~98% coverage).

Why is conference data lagging?
OpenAlex conference ingestion lags ~2 years behind Crossref. We use Crossref as a fallback for missing years.

How can I contribute?
The venue registry (data/areas.json) and affiliation mappings are open to community contributions via GitHub.
