Electrical & Computer Engineering Rankings

A metrics-based ranking of institutions by publications in selective electrical and computer engineering journals and conferences. Each paper is credited to the institutions its authors listed on that paper, split evenly among authors. It ranks institutions, not ECE departments or faculty rosters. How this is computed.
