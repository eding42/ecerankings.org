About ECERankings

ECERankings.org is a metrics-based ranking of Electrical & Computer Engineering programs, modeled on CSRankings but built for how ECE publishes — journal-heavy, IEEE-centric. Data sourced from OpenAlex and Crossref, both CC0.

Methodology

  • Adjusted counts: each paper is worth 1.0, split evenly across its N authors (1/N per author). Institution credit = sum over its authors’ shares.
  • Geometric mean: institution score = geometric mean of (1 + adjusted count) across selected areas — rewards breadth, resists single-area dominance.
  • Selective venues: only publications in the curated venue list count. A venue joins an area only if it passes both selectivity and scope tests.
  • Education-only: only authors with education-type affiliations are credited. Industry labs, government agencies, and nonprofits are excluded.
  • At Time of Publication: papers are credited to the institution(s) listed on that specific paper, not an author’s current institution.
