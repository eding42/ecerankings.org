Methodology

Credit

Each paper is worth 1, split evenly among its authors. An author's share goes to the institutions they listed on that paper, split evenly if there are several. The sum is an institution's adjusted count.

Score

The geometric mean of adjusted counts across the selected areas:

score = (∏ (1 + counti))1/n − 1

An area with no papers still counts toward n.

Scope

Whole institutions: universities, observatories and facilities, government agencies, and non-profit institutes. Companies are excluded.

Venues

29 core peer-reviewed journals across four areas: Astronomy & Astrophysics, Planetary Science, Geosciences, and Cryosphere & Climate Dynamics. Copernicus journals count peer-reviewed articles only, not discussion comments. Multidisciplinary journals such as Science count only papers in these four areas.

Data

Papers and affiliations come from OpenAlex. Modeled on CSRankings.
