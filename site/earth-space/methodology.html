Methodology

Credit

Each paper is worth 1, split evenly among its authors. An author's share goes to the institutions they listed on that paper, split evenly if there are several. The sum is an institution's adjusted count.

Score

The geometric mean of adjusted counts across the selected areas:

score = (∏ (1 + counti))1/n − 1

An area with no papers still counts toward n.

Institutional Scope

Rankings evaluate whole research institutions across academia, national observatories, space flight centers, geological surveys, and polar/oceanographic institutes (types: education, facility, government, and nonprofit). Commercial corporations (company) are excluded.

Venue Selection & Filters

29 core peer-reviewed journals across four areas: Astronomy & Astrophysics, Planetary Science, Geosciences, and Cryosphere & Climate Dynamics. Interactive open-access journals (Copernicus) are strictly filtered to peer-reviewed articles (excluding discussion comments), and multidisciplinary flagships like Science are sliced to verified subfield topics to prevent non-space contamination.

Data

Bibliographic metadata and institutional affiliations are harvested directly from OpenAlex. Modeled on CSRankings.
