Limits

  • The current calendar year is partial. Earlier years can also have incomplete indexing or affiliation data.
  • Machine Learning remains off by default. Proceedings membership and coverage are not fully verified. Paper-specific affiliations are used where recoverable; author-profile affiliations are withheld from historical credit.
  • Institution identity, branch-campus classification and duplicate provider records remain sources of uncertainty. Corrections are recorded separately from source observations.
  • Institutions with unresolved geography remain in World results and are omitted from country and region filters.
