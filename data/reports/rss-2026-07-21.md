# Collection report: rss — 2026-07-21

## Summary
Robotics: Science and Systems (RSS) identity is confirmed with high confidence via
Crossref: DOI prefix `10.15607` is registered exactly to the "Robotics: Science and
Systems Foundation" (Crossref member 6920) — no lookalike ambiguity. However, RSS is
**not usably indexed as a source in OpenAlex** — it does not fragment into per-year
proceedings sources the way IEDM/DAC/ISCA do. The only two OpenAlex sources matching
the name are near-empty: a "conference"-type source with 29 stray, DOI-less works
scattered across 2006-2020 (~2% of true output, not per-year, not representative),
and a "journal"-type source (ISSN 2330-7668, correctly identified as RSS's registered
ISSN) with **zero** works attached. All ~1,469 real RSS papers exist in OpenAlex only
as "orphaned" DOI-resolved records with `primary_location.source = null` — reachable
by DOI lookup, not by any source filter. Year coverage 2016-2025 is fully confirmed
via Crossref with DOI-prefix-verified per-year counts, all within or near the expected
volume range. The blocking problem is **affiliation data**: Crossref's RSS records
have 0% author affiliation strings (empty `affiliation: []` on every sampled author,
recent and old alike) — unlike IEDM, there is no free-text data for
`normalize_affiliations.py` to work from. The orphaned OpenAlex records do carry some
resolved institutions, but coverage is only ~45-50% on a 65-work sample (2021-2025),
and inconsistent per-paper (0/N to N/N) — the same "unreliable, not worth building
around" pattern PLAN.md already documented for orphaned records generally. **This is
the actual reason for `candidate` status, not the 85% affiliation-coverage checklist
threshold** — that check is explicitly scoped to source-linked "OpenAlex-native"
years, RSS has none, and per the skill that condition alone would be skipped and
non-blocking. The real, blocking issue is affirmative and venue-specific: for a
normal Crossref-lag venue like IEDM, deferring to `candidate` is safe because
Crossref's free-text affiliations give `normalize_affiliations.py` something to
resolve later. For RSS that premise doesn't hold — Crossref affiliations are empty,
and the only fallback is data already rejected project-wide as unreliable.
"Verified" is meant to assert "we know how to get every in-window year's data" — for
the institution attribution that is this project's entire purpose, that isn't true
yet for RSS. Identity, year-coverage, and volume all pass cleanly. Window applied:
2016-2025 (default 10-year), per task instructions; 2026 checked and confirmed not
yet published.

## Needs human review
1. **Affiliation-data source gap — needs a methodology decision.** Phase 1b's design
   (`normalize_affiliations.py`) assumes Crossref supplies free-text
   `author[].affiliation[].name` strings to normalize (as it does for IEDM). For RSS,
   Crossref's affiliation field is **empty on every sampled work**, old and new. The
   only institution signal is OpenAlex's orphaned (unsourced) DOI records, which
   PLAN.md's Phase 1b section already tested generally and rejected as "inconsistent
   ... not reliable enough to build a special path around" — confirmed again here
   with a fresh, RSS-specific sample (50.0% coverage, ranging 0/N to N/N per paper).
   Before harvesting RSS, the user should decide: (a) accept degraded/partial
   institution credit for this venue, sourced from the orphaned OpenAlex records
   despite the known unreliability, (b) look for a third data source (e.g. Semantic
   Scholar API, or scraping author lists from the official RSS proceedings site,
   which is outside this skill's and OpenAlex-ground-rules' scope), or (c) exclude
   RSS from the ranking pending better data. This is a real, quantified problem, not
   routine Crossref-fallback lag — flagging per skill step 4's "anything else you
   can't close out."
2. **`S4306420803` and `S4393918458` should not be registered as `openalex_ids`** —
   see rationale above (non-representative, DOI-less stray works; zero-works empty
   journal record). Recommend `openalex_ids: []`.
3. No lookalike-venue confusion found: "Science Robotics" (S4210213233, a Nature/AAAS
   journal) and a couple of unrelated regional-conference "Robotics ... Science ..."
   hits surfaced in broad searches but are clearly distinct and were excluded; no
   "Really Simple Syndication" or regional-symposium collisions on the "RSS" acronym
   turned up in source search (OpenAlex source search matched on the full name, not
   the acronym, so this risk didn't materialize in practice).
4. 2016 count (47) falls below `expected_per_year` [70,170] — plausible given RSS was
   a smaller conference a decade ago and 2016 sits right at the edge of the requested
   window; not treated as an anomaly, just noted per the volume-sanity check.
