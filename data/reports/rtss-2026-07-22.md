## Needs human review

One item: the `doi_prefix` correction for 2019/2024/2025 (see table above)
mutates registry values from the prior session, so it's worth a second look
before harvesting. For each of those three years, the fuzzy Crossref
container-title search — which reliably surfaces every real RTSS paper for
a year because they all share the identical `container-title` and cluster
at the top of relevance ranking — returned exactly **one** DOI prefix
(never the old, registered one), and a sample DOI under the new prefix
resolves (via `GET /works/{doi}`) to the correct RTSS edition and event
dates. That single-prefix-per-year result is what makes this a
replacement, not a merge (i.e., no second real prefix is being dropped).
Practical implication: `harvest_crossref.py` validates every DOI against
the registered `doi_prefix`, so the old (wrong) prefixes for 2019/2024/2025
would have caused every work in those years to fail validation and harvest
as empty. Use the corrected prefixes in the proposed entry below for any
future harvest of those three years.

Everything else — identity, year coverage, volume sanity, and the
not-yet-evaluable affiliation/institution checks — resolves cleanly under
the current verified definition with no open questions.
