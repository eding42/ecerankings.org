## Summary

IEEE INFOCOM is confirmed as a single logical venue with one native OpenAlex
per-year source (2022, `S4363607980`) plus Crossref DOI-prefix coverage for
every other in-window year. Re-ran the existing Crossref mapping end-to-end
for 2017–2025 and extended it to 2026 (year window for this task: 2017–2026).

registry's `expected_per_year` upper bound (380) — flagged for review, not
treated as a wrong-source signal (identity/DOI-prefix/event-name all check
out cleanly). Status: **verified**.

## Needs human review

1. **2025 `doi_prefix` correction**: registry had `10.1109/infocom54281.2025`,
   which resolves to nothing under that prefix in Crossref (confirmed by
   direct query — 0 hits). The real 2025 prefix is
   `10.1109/infocom55648.2025` (272 papers, matching the registry's existing
   count). Likely a copy/transcription slip from an earlier draft. Fixed in
   the proposed `years` block below.
2. **2026 volume anomaly**: 603 papers vs. `expected_per_year` [200, 380] —
   ~59% over the upper bound. Identity and DOI-prefix checks pass cleanly
   (single event, no duplicate DOIs), so this is not flagged as a
   wrong-source signal, but it's a large enough jump from history (max prior
   was 315 in 2018) that a human might want to confirm nothing structural
   changed (e.g., IEEE folding a companion track into the main proceedings
   DOI prefix this year) before widening `expected_per_year` to match.
3. **Lookalikes rejected**: `S4306419243` "International Conference on
   Computer Communications" (453 works, despite matching the search term)
   is NOT IEEE INFOCOM — sampled titles are Japanese-language ICT-trend
   magazine articles and unrelated telecom-policy pieces spanning 1920–2021
   with no DOIs. `S4306418035` "Conference on Computer Communications
   Workshops" (1 work) is negligible/unrelated. `S4363608785` "2008
   Proceedings IEEE INFOCOM" (311 works, an old per-year source, out of the
   2017–2026 window) was noted but not used. All Crossref workshop prefixes
   (`infocomwkshps...`, one per year, e.g. `infocomwkshps54753.2022`,
   `infocomwkshps65812.2025`) were spot-checked and confirmed distinct from
   the main-conference prefix — exclusion is correct and consistent across
   years, not just 2022.

Otherwise: none.
