(S4306420609, "Neural Information Processing Systems") captures only ~15% of

source in OpenAlex or Crossref. Status remains **candidate** — pre-2022 gap

## Year-coverage gaps

**2016-2021**: S4306420609 covers 223-731 papers/yr — far below the expected
2000-4800. These years appear to be missing ~70-85% of actual NeurIPS papers
with no discoverable source. (These papers exist on proceedings.neurips.cc
without DOIs, so they're not in Crossref either.) This is a genuine gap.

**2022-2024**: Fully covered by Crossref (not available in any OpenAlex source
with institution data). Volumes 35-37 registered with exact container-title
"Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems N". Counts: 2834, 3540,
4494 — all within expected [2000, 4800].

**2025 (Vol 38)**: Not yet registered in Crossref as of 2026-07-22. NeurIPS
2025 was held Dec 2025; this is likely an expected ingestion lag.

**2026**: N/A (conference not yet held; NeurIPS 2026 scheduled Dec 2026).

## Needs human review

1. **Pre-2022 paper gap**: ~70-85% of NeurIPS papers from 2016-2021 are not
   discoverable in OpenAlex or Crossref. S4306420609 captures only papers that
   have MAG IDs (no DOIs). The remaining papers exist at proceedings.neurips.cc
   but without DOIs. Possible approaches: (a) harvest from proceedings.neurips.cc
   directly, (b) accept the partial S4306420609 coverage as best available,
   (c) restrict ranking to 2022+ years where Crossref coverage is complete.
   Decision needed from user before promoting to verified.

2. **2022+ orphan works**: Papers from 2022-2024 exist in OpenAlex as individual
   works (with OpenAlex IDs and DOIs) but have no source linking and no
   institution data. They're usable via Crossref harvest but can't supply
   OpenAlex-native affiliation data. If the project depends on OpenAlex
   institution resolution, these years can only contribute after
   harvest_crossref.py processes them.

3. **S4306420609 affiliation coverage**: 2021 sample shows 75.8% (below 85%
   threshold); 2020 shows 91.2%. Variable quality on the partial data.

4. **No OpenAlex-native year exists for institution sniff of 2022+**: The 2021
   data from S4306420609 was used instead as the best available proxy.
