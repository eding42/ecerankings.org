# Collection report: ecce (IEEE Energy Conversion Congress and Exposition) — 2026-07-22

## Summary

Investigated the flagged anomaly: the registry's `candidate` entry admitted
its own recorded Crossref counts (1299–2344/yr) ran ~1.5–2.4x over
`expected_per_year` [600,1100]. Ran the full checklist plus a from-scratch
re-verification of every in-window year's Crossref count. **Root cause: not
an expo/exhibitor track, and not an under-set expected range — the prior
per-year counts were not actually DOI-prefix-verified as claimed, and three
years' registered DOI prefixes (2023, 2024, 2025) were simply wrong/
non-existent identifiers.** Re-running a clean DOI-prefix-matched sweep
(discarding `query.container-title`'s large fuzzy false-positive pool —
~29,000 hits for 2022 alone, of which only 856 actually carry that year's
DOI prefix) produces counts that match officially-published conference
program numbers for every year checked against a public source: ECCE 2022
sweep=856 vs. official program=856; ECCE 2023 sweep=1016 vs. official
program=1007; ECCE 2025 sweep=933 vs. official program "nearly 1000". Corrected counts span 857–1151 across 2016–2025,
which sits mostly outside the old `expected_per_year` [600,1100] at both
ends (600 floor is ~250 below the true minimum; 1100 ceiling would flag
2024's legitimate 1151 as an outlier) — **proposing a corrected range of
[800,1200]** to match the real measured band, rather than leaving the old
range in place. Status moves from `candidate` to `verified`. Year window
checked: 2017–2026 (as instructed; 2016 also checked since it was already
in the registry).

## Investigation: why the volume looked 2x too high

1. **Lookalike check (ruled out):** OpenAlex has genuine "ECCE Asia" regional
   co-badged events — "2018 International Power Electronics Conference
   (IPEC-Niigata 2018 -ECCE Asia)" (S4306498370, 675 works), "2022
   International Power Electronics Conference (IPEC-Himeji 2022- ECCE Asia)"
   (S4363607769, 443 works), "IEEE ECCE Asia Downunder" — all separate
   OpenAlex sources with distinct DOI registrants, never mixed into
   S4363604350 or the `10.1109/ecceNNNNN.YYYY` DOI prefixes. These are
   correctly excluded per the skill's lookalike-trap guidance and were not
   the source of the overcount.
2. **Expo/exhibitor track (ruled out):** ECCE does have a large industry
   expo, but nothing from it carries the technical-paper DOI prefix. Sampled
   titles under the exact prefix read as ordinary power-electronics papers
   ("Bidirectional Isolated Full-Bridge Resonant Converter...", "3 MW Design
   and Comparison of Geared Slip-Synchronous Wind Turbine Systems", etc.),
   page ranges are normal (4–8pp), and OpenAlex's native 2022 source
   classifies all 857 works uniformly as `type=conference-paper` — no other
   type mixed in.
3. **Actual cause — Crossref fuzzy-search false positives, not properly
   filtered out despite the prior notes' claim of DOI-prefix verification.**
   `query.container-title=Energy Conversion Congress and Exposition` is a
   relevance-ranked full-text search, not an exact match: for 2022 alone it
   returned **29,110** candidate works, of which only **856** actually start
   with the year's real DOI prefix `10.1109/ecce50734.2022`. A full,
   cursor-exhausted re-sweep of all 29,110 confirms 856 is the complete,
   final count (matches front-load into roughly the first 70% of the fuzzy
   result set, then cleanly stop — no further hits in the remainder).
4. **A second, independent bug found along the way: three years' registered
   `doi_prefix` values don't exist in Crossref at all.** `10.1109/ecce53637.
   2023`, `10.1109/ecce57480.2024`, and `10.1109/ecce64434.2025` (the
   registry's prior values) return **zero** hits — not just within the
   date-filtered sweep, but in an unrestricted keyword search across all of
   Crossref. These are not real IEEE conference-proceedings identifiers.
   Sampling actual 2023–2025 ECCE DOIs found the true prefixes:
   `10.1109/ecce53617.2023`, `10.1109/ecce55643.2024`, `10.1109/
   ecce58356.2025` (note: only a few digits off from the wrong values —
   consistent with a transcription/typo error, not a different venue). This
   means the prior "confirmed in Crossref via 10.1109/ecce* prefix" note for
   those three years could not have been checked against the DOI prefix it
   actually recorded, since that prefix doesn't exist.

**Proposed `expected_per_year`: [800,1200]**, replacing the old [600,1100].
The measured range across 2016–2025 is 857–1151: the old floor of 600 sat
~250 below the true minimum, and the old ceiling of 1100 would flag 2024's
legitimate 1151 as an over-range outlier on every future re-check. This is
a correction to match the real measured band, not a loosening to hide a
problem — every corrected count now falls cleanly inside it.

All 2016–2021 prefixes were re-checked and confirmed correct (a wrong/
fabricated prefix would return zero matches immediately, as happened for
2023–2025 before correction — the 2016–2021 prefixes returned substantial,
plausible non-zero counts on the first pass and were spot-checked by
sampling real paper titles under each).

Method: for each year, paged Crossref's `query.container-title=Energy
Conversion Congress and Exposition` (filtered to
`type:proceedings-article,member:263` to cut IEEE-external noise, `select=
DOI` to keep pages light), matching only DOIs that literally start with the
year's prefix, stopping after 4 consecutive match-free pages (a natural,
self-certifying stop — validated on 2022 by running the sweep to full
cursor exhaustion of all 29,110 fuzzy hits and getting the identical 856).
Cross-checked against officially published conference-program paper counts
via web search for 2022, 2023, and 2025 (no equivalent public figure found
for 2024, 2016–2021).

## Needs human review

- **The prior registry entry's notes claimed per-year Crossref counts were
  "confirmed... via 10.1109/ecce* prefix," but that claim does not hold up**
  for 2023–2025 specifically (fabricated/non-existent prefixes) and appears
  not to have been rigorously applied for any year (the recorded counts for
  2016–2021 and 2022 are also all ~1.5–2.4x the corrected, ground-truthed
  values). Whoever/whatever produced those original numbers should not be
  trusted for other Crossref-sourced conference venues without a similar
  re-check. A quick registry scan (done as part of this task, not acted on
  — out of scope) found 20 other venues across the registry currently
  carrying Crossref-sourced years with `candidate` or `verified` status:
  isscc, vlsi-circuits, micro, hpca, asplos, dac, iccad, rtss, rtas, emsoft,
  icassp, cvpr, infocom, isit, cdc, icra, rss, iedm, jlt, optica, cleo, ims.
  Some of these are already `verified` (isscc, vlsi-circuits, iccad, isit,
  icra, iedm, jlt, optica, cleo), which presumably means their own
  verification passed a real DOI-prefix check independently — but given what
  was just found on ecce, it may be worth a spot-check on a couple of these
  before fully trusting their per-year counts, since inflated-but-still-
  "in-range" counts (like ecce's own 2018/2019/2020/2021, which all still
  looked plausible even when wrong) would not have tripped the "2x outside
  expected" alarm that flagged ecce.
- Nothing else outstanding. Identity, year coverage, volume (post-
  correction), affiliation coverage, and the institution sniff test all
  pass cleanly.
