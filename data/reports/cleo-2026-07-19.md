# Collection report: cleo — 2026-07-19

## Summary

Full source resolution + verification for flagship CLEO (Conference on Lasers
and Electro-Optics), scoped to the default 10-year window **2016–2025**.
Unlike most flagship IEEE conferences in this registry, CLEO is **not**
fragmented into per-year OpenAlex sources — it lives under a single stable
series-level source, `S4306418073` ("Conference on Lasers and
Electro-Optics"), which covers 2016–2022 (7 of 10 in-window years) plus a
long back-history to 1973. 2023–2025 are absent from OpenAlex (conference
ingestion lag, consistent with the pattern already seen on IEDM/ISSCC/DAC),
but confirmed present in Crossref via DOI-prefix-verified counts
(1704/1907/1553 respectively). The venue's 3 tracks (Applications &
Technology, QELS Fundamental Science, Science & Innovations) are **not**
split into separate OpenAlex sources — all three fold into the one source ID,
distinguished only by DOI sub-prefix (`cleo_at`, `cleo_qels`, `cleo_si`).
Identity, affiliation coverage (86–99.5% across 3 sampled years), and
institution sniff test all pass cleanly. Volume is very high (1550–2710
papers/yr), one year (2019) running ~35% above the registry's
`expected_per_year` upper bound — flagged for a future tier-placement call,
not decided here. Status set to `candidate`, not `verified`, matching the
IEDM precedent: the 3 Crossref-sourced years are identity/volume-confirmed
but haven't gone through `harvest_crossref.py` / `normalize_affiliations.py`,
which don't exist yet (Phase 1b).

## Year-coverage gaps

No genuine gaps in the 2016–2025 window — every year has data from one
source or the other.

- **2023, 2024, 2025**: absent from OpenAlex (`S4306418073`'s
  `group_by=publication_year` has zero entries for these years — checked
  directly, not just "not in top results"). This is the expected ~2-year
  conference-ingestion lag documented in `PLAN.md` and the skill. Crossref
  has all three: verified by fetching every `type:proceedings-article` work
  under Optica's `10.1364` DOI prefix for each year (5113/5538/4906 raw
  candidates per year, since `10.1364` covers all Optica-published
  conferences, not just CLEO) and filtering to `container-title == "CLEO
  20XX"` **and** DOI starting `10.1364/cleo_` — yielding 1704 (2023), 1907
  (2024), 1553 (2025) flagship CLEO papers, cleanly separated from
  co-located Optica meetings (OFC, FiO+LS, Biophotonics Congress, etc., which
  appear in the same raw candidate pool under different `container-title`
  values). This is the same ingestion-lag pattern already seen on
  IEDM/ISSCC/DAC/ISCA — expected, not a real gap; will need Phase 1b
  (`harvest_crossref.py`, not yet built) to actually pull these years' paper
  data.

## Needs human review

- **Volume / tier placement**: recent-year counts (1553–2710/yr) sit at or
  above the registry's `expected_per_year: [1500, 2000]`, and 2019 (2710) is
  ~35% over the upper bound — within the skill's "worth a note" band, not the
  "probably wrong source" band (2×). This confirms the existing registry note
  ("very large — likely tier-2 or excluded") is directionally right, but the
  actual tier/inclusion call belongs to a future methodology decision, not
  this report.
- **`expected_per_year` may need updating**: given confirmed volume across
  10 years (1553–2710), consider whether `[1500, 2000]` should widen — flagged
  for the person making the tier-placement call above, not changed here.
- **Status `candidate` not `verified`**: consistent with the IEDM precedent —
  Crossref-covered years (2023–2025) are identity/volume-confirmed via DOI
  prefix but have not gone through the not-yet-built Phase 1b pipeline
  (`harvest_crossref.py`, `normalize_affiliations.py`). Re-run verification
  once those scripts exist and this venue can likely move to `verified`
  without redoing the source-resolution work above.
- **Rejected lookalikes** (for the record, none included):
  - `S4363607729` "Conference on Lasers and Electro-Optics Europe" and
    `S4363607725` "1994 Conference on Lasers and Electro-Optics Europe" —
    CLEO/Europe-EQEC, a separate regional conference. Excluded per explicit
    instruction.
  - `S4306420444` "Lasers and Electro-Optics Society Meeting" — the old IEEE
    LEOS Annual Meeting, a different (predecessor-era) meeting, not CLEO.
    Excluded.
  - `S4363608470` "2007 Conference on Lasers and Electro-Optics (CLEO)" — a
    one-off source holding only 2007 (1445 works), evidently a stray
    split-out of that single year from the main series source. Outside the
    2016–2025 window regardless; noted here only as evidence CLEO *has*
    fragmented in isolated past years even though the 2016–2025 window
    happens to be clean.
  - **CLEO-PR (CLEO Pacific Rim)**: does not exist as an OpenAlex source at
    all — searches for "CLEO Pacific Rim", "CLEO-PR", and "Conference on
    Lasers and Electro-Optics Pacific Rim" all returned zero results. Its
    Crossref DOIs (`10.1109/cleo-pr...`, IEEE-hosted, distinct from flagship
    CLEO's Optica `10.1364/cleo_...` DOIs) confirm it's a genuinely separate
    conference with different publisher/DOI namespace, and a DOI-prefix
    sample of ~1400 works from `S4306418073` across 2016–2022 found zero
    CLEO-PR or CLEO/Europe contamination inside the main source. No action
    needed, but noting it was actively checked rather than assumed absent.
- **Track-splitting question (resolved)**: the registry notes asked whether
  CLEO's 3 tracks (A&T, Fundamental Science/QELS, Science & Innovations) are
  split into separate OpenAlex sources per track per year. Answer: **no** —
  confirmed via DOI sub-prefix breakdown on a sample of ~1400 works spanning
  2016–2022, all under the one source `S4306418073`, split only by DOI
  substring (`cleo_at`, `cleo_qels`, `cleo_si`, plus a small number of bare
  `cleo.` DOIs for cross-track/plenary sessions).
