# Collection report: ICCV (Image Processing & Vision area) — 2026-07-22

## Summary

ICCV (IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision) is confirmed
**genuinely biennial, odd years only** — not transitioning to annual. ICCV
2027 is already announced (Oct 2–8, Hong Kong), consistent with the
2011/2013/.../2025 odd-year cadence. Within the requested 2017–2026 window,
ICCV was held in **2017, 2019, 2021, 2023, 2025**; **2018, 2020, 2022, 2024,
2026 are legitimate N/A years, not gaps** (conference simply didn't convene).

Exactly one year (2021) has a native OpenAlex per-year source
(`S4363607764`, 1617 works, matching CVPR's same one-native-year pattern).
The other four held years (2017, 2019, 2023, 2025) are absent from OpenAlex
as linked sources but fully present in Crossref with clean, DOI-prefix-
verified counts — a per-year checksum confirmed by sampling and paging
until the true-match rate dropped to zero. All five held years therefore
have a confirmed data source; status is **verified**. Affiliation coverage
on the one OpenAlex-native year (2021) is 92.6%, well above the 85%
threshold, and the institution sniff test surfaces exactly the expected mix
(Chinese Academy of Sciences, Tsinghua, CUHK, Zhejiang, CMU, plus Google/
Meta/Microsoft/Alibaba/Huawei industry labs) for a flagship vision venue.
This is a CS-overlap venue — default-off tier-2 toggle per PLAN.md,
Workshops proceedings excluded throughout.
