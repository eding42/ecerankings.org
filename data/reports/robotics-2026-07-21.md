## Needs human review

1. **rss institution attribution gap (the main open item):** RSS has no
   native OpenAlex source, 0% Crossref affiliation strings, and only
   ~45-50% coverage on OpenAlex's orphaned Crossref-ingested records — the
   project's core purpose (institution attribution) isn't currently solvable
   for this venue with the existing pipeline. Options per the worker's
   report: (a) accept degraded orphaned-record coverage and harvest anyway,
   (b) look for a third data source (Semantic Scholar, RSS's own proceedings
   site), or (c) leave RSS as `candidate`/unharvested pending a better
   source. This needs an explicit decision — not something to guess into the
   registry.
2. **icra rejected/contaminated sources, flagged for awareness only (already
   handled, not blocking):** `S4306419799` ("International Conference on
   Robotics and Automation" catch-all, 698 works 1984–2021, 8–61/yr) was
   rejected as contaminated with unrelated papers. A DOI-prefix lookalike
   (ICRAI, `10.1109/icrai...`) was caught and excluded from the 2026 count
   after initially producing 55 false hits — fixed before it reached the
   registry, noted here for the record.
