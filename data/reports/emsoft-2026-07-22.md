## Year-coverage gaps

- **2017 — RESOLVED, not a gap.** Main-track EMSOFT 2017 papers were published
  in *ACM Transactions on Embedded Computing Systems* (TECS) **16(5s)**, a
  joint special issue titled "ESWEEK 2017, CASES 2017, CODES+ISSS 2017 and
  EMSOFT 2017." dblp's TOC for that issue sub-divides it into three
  conference-tagged sections (CASES: 18 papers, CODES+ISSS: 27 papers, EMSOFT:
  28 papers). The EMSOFT-only subset's DOIs form a **contiguous, gap-free
  range**: `10.1145/3126495` – `10.1145/3126522` (28 unique DOIs, verified no
  missing numbers in that span). Confirmed by sampling: titles and author
  affiliations (Univ. of Turku, Aalto, Auckland, Kiel, etc.) read as
  legitimate embedded/CPS research, matching the field and roughly the size of
  neighboring IEEE years (2016: 27, 2018: 21).
- **2019 — RESOLVED, not a gap.** Same pattern: main-track EMSOFT 2019 papers
  are in TECS **18(5s)**, joint issue shared with CASES 2019 (16 papers) and
  CODES+ISSS 2019 (~22 papers); EMSOFT-only subset = 27 papers, DOI range
  `10.1145/3358213` – `10.1145/3358239`, contiguous, no gaps. Sampled titles
  (ReachNN, worst-case STL satisfaction w/ neural controllers) and affiliations
  (Northwestern, Boston University, ASU) confirm identity.
- **Why this wasn't found the first time**: the original search likely used
  `query.container-title="International Conference on Embedded Software"` or
  similar — that string only matches the *Companion* volumes for 2017/2019
  ("... 2017 Companion", "... Embedded Software Companion"), which contain WIP
  papers only, not the joint-issue container title
  ("ACM Transactions on Embedded Computing Systems"), so the real main-track
  papers were invisible to that query shape.
- **2026 — genuinely not yet available**, not a defect. EMSOFT/ESWEEK 2026 is
  confirmed on the calendar (Oct 4–9, 2026, Barcelona) via IEEE CAS and SIGARCH
  event listings and the live HotCRP submission site (emsoft26.hotcrp.com).
  Checked Crossref for 2026 EMSOFT proceedings — none exist yet (expected,
  conference hasn't run). **Notable for future collection**: EMSOFT's CFP
  states 2026 full papers will publish in **IEEE Transactions on
  Computer-Aided Design of Integrated Circuits and Systems (TCAD)**, a further
  shift away from the `10.1109/emsoft*` / `10.1145/*` DOI patterns used
  2016–2025. Whoever collects 2026 data should search TCAD, not an
  `emsoft`-branded proceedings DOI.

## Declining paper-count trend — explained, not a Crossref artifact

With 2017 and 2019 filled in, the full run is:
2016: 27, 2017: 28, 2018: 21, 2019: 27, 2020: 16, 2021: 15, 2022: 19,
2023: 9, 2024: 10, 2025: 8.

This is **not** an indexing/coverage artifact — the counts are all
DOI-verified. There is a real decline, but it's more gradual than the raw
27→8 headline suggested (2016–2019 cluster around 21–28; the drop to
single-digit/mid-teens happens from 2020 onward). Root cause is out of scope
to investigate further here (methodology, not data-collection), but plausibly
relates to EMSOFT tightening to a more selective/shorter format, or full
papers migrating toward journal-integrated venues (TECS special issues in
2017/2019, and now TCAD from 2026) which may affect submission volume
differently than a standalone conference CFP.

## Needs human review

1. **TECS double-count risk — the most important flag in this report.** This
   area's `tecs` entry (`data/areas.json`, area `embedded`) registers
   `S136160450` ("ACM Transactions on Embedded Computing Systems") as its own
   verified journal venue, harvested by `publication_year`. **The EMSOFT
   2017/2019 papers this report adds are literally TECS 16(5s) / 18(5s)
   journal articles under that same source ID** — a plain `publication_year:2017`
   / `publication_year:2019` harvest of `tecs` will already pull these 28 + 27
   papers in (native OpenAlex, not Crossref-only, for the `tecs` venue's own
   harvest path). If `emsoft`'s 2017/2019 years are *also* harvested (via
   Crossref, per their `years{}` entries below), **the same ~55 papers will be
   double-counted across the `tecs` and `emsoft` venues** in any downstream
   aggregation — inflating both an institution's and an author's raw paper
   count for those two years. This needs a decision before Phase 1b harvesting
   runs for either venue's 2017/2019: either (a) exclude these DOIs from
   `tecs`'s harvest for 2017/2019, (b) exclude them from `emsoft`'s harvest and
   accept `tecs` absorbs them (methodologically odd, since they're EMSOFT
   papers), or (c) de-duplicate by DOI at aggregation time regardless of which
   venue attributed them. Not resolved here — registering the entry as
   `verified` is still correct (identity/coverage/volume checks all pass), but
   this is a live aggregation-correctness issue, not just a note.
2. **Schema/harvest-ability for 2017/2019**: registered as explicit `dois`
   lists (all 28 + 27 DOIs spelled out), not a prefix or range — more robust
   than a numeric range (immune to a future erratum landing inside the range)
   and directly harvestable (`pipeline/harvest_crossref.py` can fetch each DOI
   rather than needing new range-matching logic). This is a schema
   augmentation beyond the existing `doi_prefix` convention — orchestrator/
   maintainer should confirm this shape works for Phase 1b before relying on
   it, and reconcile it with flag #1 above (whichever DOIs get excluded from
   `tecs` or `emsoft` should be excluded consistently).
3. **Lookalike rejected**: OpenAlex source `S4306418261` ("Embedded Software",
   conference type, 161 works, spanning 1991–2021 non-contiguously) looked at
   first glance like it might be the elusive EMSOFT catch-all. Sampled
   several years (2011, 2013, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2021) — pre-2016 years
   are genuine EMSOFT main-track papers (DOI prefix `10.5555/...`, ACM's
   legacy no-DOI-registered prefix), but 2017/2018/2019/2021 samples are
   **all "Work-in-Progress:" / companion / special-session papers** — i.e.
   this source is (or degrades into, post-2016) the EMSOFT **Companion/WIP**
   track, a companion volume per the skill's lookalike-trap rules, not the
   main conference. Correctly **excluded** from `openalex_ids`.
4. **Confidence on the 2017/2019 counts**: high — cross-validated via two
   independent sources (Crossref DOI existence + dblp's sub-section paper
   listing with matching counts), and the DOI lists have zero gaps in their
   numeric span. No further review needed on correctness, just on flags #1/#2
   above.
5. Everything else: no other ambiguities. 2020–2025 years were left
   untouched (already correctly registered per the input state) since only
   2017/2019/2026 were in scope for this re-check.
