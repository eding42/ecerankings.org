## Year-coverage gaps
None within 2016–2026. Every edition has a Crossref-covered, DOI-prefix-validated
count. Confirmed (again) no native OpenAlex per-year source exists for any
year: searched `/sources` for "ASPLOS" and the full spelled-out name; the only
hit was `S4306417705` ("Architectural Support for Programming Languages and
Operating Systems", 16 works). Inspecting its works directly showed titles
from 1988, 1991, 1992, 2000, 2009–2012, 2014, 2016, 2019 with no
`primary_location.source` set on the sample works and no coherent per-year
structure — a legacy/orphan-aggregation artifact, not a real proceedings
source. This matches and reconfirms the existing registry note.

**2026 edition added**: `10.1145/3760250` (Volume 1, published 2025-12-11 —
ASPLOS's two-deadline submission model means Volume 1 is deposited to
Crossref months before the conference itself, same pattern already handled
by the registry's edition-year bucketing for 2023–2025) and `10.1145/3779212`
(Volume 2, published 2026-03-22, the conference dates). Confirmed via
`dblp.org/db/conf/asplos/asplos2026-{1,2}.html` that ASPLOS 2026 has exactly
2 volumes (down from 4 in 2023/2024, 3 in 2025) — verified against Crossref

Six of nine spot-checked years match exactly (three of those matches include
raw front-matter counts, meaning the registered number and mine both include
"Session details: ..." entries that Crossref types as `proceedings-article`
but aren't real papers). 2016 and 2018 came back lower than registered in my
recount even at `rows=1000` (ruled out simple pagination truncation); 2025
came back *higher*. I could not determine which number is more accurate for
these three years using only the Crossref fuzzy-search API — per SKILL.md's
own caveat, `query.container-title` is relevance-ranked, not an exhaustive
filter, and can both under- and over-surface entries depending on how a
paper's metadata happens to score against the query text. This is a
data-quality note about count precision, not an identity or coverage
failure — it doesn't block `verified` (the skill's checklist doesn't require
exact paper-count reproduction, only that the year is covered by *some*
DOI-verified source and passes the ~30%-tolerance volume-sanity check, which
every one of these years does either way). Flagged below for a future
authoritative recount via `harvest_crossref.py`'s exhaustive cursor-paged
pull.

## Needs human review
- **2016, 2018, 2025 count discrepancies** (table above): my from-scratch
  Crossref recount didn't match the registered count in either direction.
  Recommend treating all registry `count` values as provisional pending
  `harvest_crossref.py`'s exhaustive DOI-verified pull (Phase 1b), which will
  produce an authoritative figure by paging fully rather than relying on
  relevance-ranked search.
- **Front-matter inflation in 2017 and 2019**: Crossref types "Session
  details: ..." entries as `proceedings-article` (17 such entries in 2017, 24
  in 2019) — these are not papers and will need to be filtered by title
  pattern (not just DOI-prefix + type) when `harvest_crossref.py` actually
  harvests these years, or they'll pollute the paper-level dataset with
  authorless/non-scholarly records. Worth checking whether other years have
  a smaller number of these too (I only found them in 2017/2019 of the years
  checked; 2020–2022, 2024 came back with zero).
- **"Menlo School" / "Seattle University" institution artifacts** in the 2025
  Vol.1 sniff sample (19 authorships each, same order of magnitude as top
  research universities) — plausibly an OpenAlex affiliation-resolution
  mismatch rather than real output from those institutions. Not
  venue-specific and not blocking, but worth a manual-override note if it
  recurs at aggregation time.
- No lookalike venues found needing rejection this pass — searches for
  "ASPLOS" and the full spelled-out name surfaced only the venue itself
  (as a junk aggregation source, not a real per-year one) plus nothing else
  plausible.
