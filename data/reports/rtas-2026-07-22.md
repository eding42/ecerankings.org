# Collection report: rtas (embedded) — 2026-07-22

## Summary

RTAS (IEEE Real-Time and Embedded Technology and Applications Symposium) has
no adequate native OpenAlex source — confirmed again this session. Both
previously-identified candidates (S4210197441, S4306420776) remain inadequate:
neither has any meaningful coverage inside the in-scope window. All 11 years
2016–2026 are confirmed via Crossref with DOI-prefix-validated, on-topic
proceedings, including the newly-checked 2026 edition (held May 2026, Saint
Malo, France — already indexed). **Re-verifying the existing Crossref mapping
turned up real errors**: 4 of the 10 previously-registered years
(2020, 2022, 2023, 2025) had an incorrect `doi_prefix` (the registered value
does not exist in Crossref at all — likely transposed from a wrong DOI or
copy/paste slip), and 4 years (2017, 2018, 2022, 2023) had slightly
off counts. Corrected values are below and in the proposed registry entry.
Under the current "verified means we know how to get every year's data"
definition, and given this is a Crossref-only conference with no native
OpenAlex year to sample for affiliation/institution checks, **status is
promoted to `verified`**.

Window applied: 2017–2026 (current year + 9 prior), per default scope; 2016
was already resolved from a prior session and is kept as a bonus backfilled
year.

Verification method: queried
`api.crossref.org/works?query.container-title=Real-Time and Embedded
Technology and Applications Symposium&filter=from-pub-date:YYYY-01-01,
until-pub-date:YYYY-12-31,type:proceedings-article&rows=1000` per year, then
grouped results by DOI prefix matching `10\.1109/rtas[a-z0-9]*\.\d{4}`. Each
corrected prefix was spot-checked against 2–3 sample records: `container-title`
reads verbatim `"20XX IEEE Nth Real-Time and Embedded Technology and
Applications Symposium (RTAS)"`, `publisher: IEEE`. 2026 additionally
confirmed via the Crossref `event` block: name matches, location Saint Malo,
France, dates 2026-05-11–14 — a real, already-held edition, not a
not-yet-scheduled placeholder.

The 4 "wrong prefix" years (2020/2022/2023/2025) are the more serious finding:
those `doi_prefix` values do not correspond to any real DOI in Crossref
(`filter=doi:<old-prefix>` returns zero results), so
`pipeline/harvest_crossref.py`, which validates every DOI against the
registered `doi_prefix`, would have silently harvested **zero papers** for
those 4 years had it been run against the old registry values. This is fixed
in the proposed entry below — worth noting since it means the prior session's
Crossref mapping work, while directionally correct (right venue, right year,
right rough count), had not actually been checked DOI-by-DOI before being
written to the registry.

The small count corrections (2017: 53→50, 2018: 43→42, 2022: 41→39,
2023: 42→41) are consistent with either front-matter items (copyright pages,
title pages — Crossref sometimes classifies these as `type:proceedings-article`
too) being counted inconsistently between runs, or the original count simply
being taken from an unvalidated total-results figure rather than a
DOI-prefix-filtered one. None of these are large enough to affect volume
sanity.
