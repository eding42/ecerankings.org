# Collection report: iclr — 2026-07-22

## Summary

Investigated OpenAlex coverage of the International Conference on Learning
Representations (ICLR). Only one source exists (S4306419637), covering a small
fraction of ICLR papers (~200-250/yr) for 2013-2021 only. No per-year
conference sources were found for 2022+; ICLR papers from these years do exist
in OpenAlex but are attributed to arXiv (S4306400194) as primary source
instead of an ICLR-specific venue. Crossref has no ICLR proceedings (ICLR uses
OpenReview, not Crossref DOIs). The 4-year gap (2022-2025) is a genuine data
gap, not an ingestion lag — ICLR's OpenReview-based publishing model is not
well captured by OpenAlex's source taxonomy. The existing source is confirmed
as the correct venue (identity, affiliation coverage, and institution sniff
all pass), but the volume is ~200/yr vs the expected 1500-3800, and we cannot
obtain every in-window year's data through either OpenAlex or Crossref.
Recommended status: **candidate** (not verifiable without an alternative data
source or a change to OpenAlex's ICLR coverage).

## Year-coverage gaps

- **2022-2025**: All four years are missing from both OpenAlex and Crossref.
   - *OpenAlex*: No per-year source found. Known ICLR 2022 papers (e.g. "Do
     Transformers Really Perform Bad for Graph Representation") exist in
     OpenAlex but are attributed to arXiv (S4306400194) as their primary
     location source, not to any ICLR-specific venue.
   - *Crossref*: ICLR does not use Crossref DOIs; its papers are hosted on
     OpenReview.net and often have arXiv IDs. Crossref queries for the venue
     name with `type:proceedings-article` return irrelevant results.
   - **Conclusion: genuine gap.** Unlike CVPR/NeurIPS (which have IEEE/ACM
     proceedings in Crossref), ICLR's OpenReview-based publishing model means
     it has no Crossref presence either. These years are not verifiable through
     either source for ICLR-specific attribution.
- **2016-2021**: Covered by the existing source S4306419637, but at severely
  low volume (~20-252/yr). The source appears to capture only a fraction of
  ICLR's actual output, likely those papers that happened to have some
  Crossref-like identifier ingested by OpenAlex.
- **2026**: The current year; no data exists yet in either source. Pending.

## Needs human review

1. **Genuine coverage gap for 2022+**: ICLR's OpenReview-based publishing
   model means its papers are not registered with Crossref and are not
   reliably attributed to an ICLR-specific source in OpenAlex. This is not a
   simple ingestion lag — it is a structural missing-data problem. The
   project's methodology (PLAN.md) may need to decide whether to:
   - Accept arXiv-attributed works as ICLR papers (using some heuristic like
     OpenReview URL matching or manual year-to-venue mapping)
   - Consider ICLR non-verifiable under current OpenAlex-based methodology and
     exclude or deprecate it
   - Add an alternative data source (e.g. OpenReview API or proceedings
     download) specifically for ICLR
2. **Volume undershoot**: The existing source's ~200-252 works/yr is far below
   the [1500, 3800] expected range. ICLR's actual acceptance has grown from
   ~500 (2018) to ~3000+ (recent years). The source appears to be sampling
   only a subset, likely those published with traditional identifiers.
3. **One known source, no alternatives found**: S4306419637 is the only
   OpenAlex source matching ICLR. No per-year or per-edition sources were
   found for any year 2016-2025 despite exhaustive search. The `autocomplete`
   endpoint also fails to surface it under "iclr" search (returns unrelated
   law journals instead).
