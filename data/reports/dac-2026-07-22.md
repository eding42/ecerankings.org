## Summary

DAC's registry entry flips from `candidate` to `verified`. Every year in the
requested 2017–2026 window has a confirmed source: 2018 and 2022 are
OpenAlex-native, the remaining seven (2017, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2023, 2024,

test all pass. One volume note: four early/mid years fall below the
registry's `expected_per_year` floor of 250 — real venue-size growth over
the decade, not a data problem (see below).

### Pre-window legacy sources (already in `openalex_ids`, now explained)

Four IDs already in the registry's `openalex_ids` array were unmapped in the
prior notes. All four are confirmed via sample-work DOI prefixes to be real
DAC editions, just outside the 2017–2026 window:

| Source ID | Title | Works | True edition year (DOI-confirmed) | OpenAlex `publication_year` metadata |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S4363608898 | "Proceedings of the 38th Design Automation Conference (IEEE Cat. No.01CH37232)" | 152 | **2001** (DOIs are `10.1109/dac.2001.*`) | Mislabeled: all 152 works tagged `publication_year=2002` |
| S4363608888 | "Proceedings 2002 Design Automation Conference (IEEE Cat. No.02CH37324)" | 170 | **2002** (DOIs are `10.1109/dac.2002.*`) | Matches: `publication_year=2002` |
| S4363608873 | "Proceedings 2003. Design Automation Conference (IEEE Cat. No.03CH37451)" | 180 | **2003** (DOIs are `10.1109/dac.2003.*`) | Mislabeled: 179/180 works tagged `publication_year=2004`, 1 tagged 2003 |
| S4363608863 | "Proceedings. 42nd Design Automation Conference, 2005." | 194 | **2005** (title + cat. no. confirm) | Matches: `publication_year=2005` |

**Harvest trap to flag for Phase 1:** if a future `--all-years` backfill for
these pre-window years ever runs `pipeline/harvest.py` with a
`publication_year:2001` (or `:2003`) filter against these source IDs, it will
return **zero** works — the true edition year and OpenAlex's internal
`publication_year` field disagree for S4363608898 and S4363608873. A correct
harvest of those two years must either omit the `publication_year` filter
entirely (pull all works from the source ID and trust the DOI-derived year)
or filter on `publication_year:2002`/`:2004` respectively while bucketing the
output under years `2001`/`2003` in the cache. This is out of the requested
window so not resolved here, only documented so it isn't rediscovered the
hard way.

None of the below-floor years are anywhere near the 2× deviation that would
signal a wrong/missing source. Notably, **2018's count (186) is
OpenAlex-native** — resolved independently of the Crossref phrase-search
method used for the Crossref years — and it's sub-250 too. That corroborates
that the low early-year counts reflect real venue size at the time (DAC's
proceedings volume has grown substantially over the decade as more
tracks/special sessions got individual DOIs), not an artifact of the
Crossref counting method. Worth flagging to whoever owns `expected_per_year`
for this venue: the current floor of 250 looks miscalibrated for 2017–2019;
not changed here since that's a registry-methodology call, not this report's.

## Needs human review

- **ASME lookalike trap (excluded, not registered):** Crossref's fuzzy
  `query.container-title` search for "Design Automation Conference" also
  surfaces ASME's unrelated **"Design Automation Conference" track within
  IDETC/CIE** (DOI prefix `10.1115/detc...`, e.g. "Volume 11B: 46th Design
  Automation Conference (DAC)") — a mechanical-engineering CAD/design-methods
  venue, completely different field from the ACM/IEEE chip-design DAC this
  registry tracks. It shares the exact string "Design Automation Conference"
  and even a "DAC" abbreviation, making naive title/keyword search
  dangerously easy to conflate. Confirmed excluded — no `10.1115` DOIs appear
  in this registry's counts — but flagging for the next person who touches
  this venue, since a less careful Crossref query could silently pull in
  ASME papers.
- **Pre-window `publication_year` mismatches** (S4363608898 → true 2001,
  metadata 2002; S4363608873 → true 2003, metadata mostly 2004) — see
  "Harvest trap" note above. Not a blocker for `verified` (out of window),
  but will need explicit handling whenever DAC's pre-2017 history is
  backfilled.
- Everything else: none. All nine in-window years have a confirmed,
  DOI-verified or OpenAlex-native source; no unresolved ambiguities.
