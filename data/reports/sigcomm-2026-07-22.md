# Collection report: sigcomm (ACM SIGCOMM) — 2026-07-22

## Summary

ACM SIGCOMM (the flagship networking conference, ACM-published, DOI prefix
`10.1145/*`) has **no usable native OpenAlex per-year source** in the 2017–2026
window. The only OpenAlex source matching "SIGCOMM" is
`S66039016` ("ACM SIGCOMM Computer Communication Review", CCR) — verified by
sampling recent-year works to be the quarterly newsletter/magazine (editorials,
"The January 2024 Issue", workshop reports), not the conference proceedings.
A second source, `S4306417580` ("ACM Special Interest Group on Data
Communication", type conference, 36 works total), is a sparse legacy
catch-all spanning 1985–2021 with only one work in the entire 2017–2026 window
(a single 2021 item) — not usable for per-year coverage. **`openalex_ids` is
therefore left empty.** All ten in-window years (2017–2026) were instead
resolved and DOI-prefix-verified in Crossref, mirroring the INFOCOM
methodology from this same run. Nine years (2017–2025) have confirmed,
DOI-validated paper counts; 2026 has zero papers because the conference
(Aug 17–21, 2026, Denver) has not yet been held as of this report's date — a
genuine future-event gap, not a data problem. One data-quality nuance found
and flagged below: 2023 and 2025 main-proceedings DOI prefixes bundle
poster/demo-session papers in with the full technical papers (a change from
2017–2022, 2024 where posters/demos always got a separate DOI prefix) —
important for future harvesting/filtering, not a verification blocker.
Status: **verified**.

## Needs human review

1. **No usable OpenAlex source at all** (unlike INFOCOM, which had one native
   2022 source). `S66039016` (CCR) was rejected as a lookalike — it's the
   quarterly magazine/newsletter, not conference proceedings (sampled 2021–2024
   titles are editorials, "issue" front-matter, and non-peer-reviewed
   community pieces, not full research papers). `S4306417580` ("ACM Special
   Interest Group on Data Communication", 36 works total, 1985–2021) was also
   rejected — sparse legacy catch-all with only a single work anywhere in the
   2017–2026 window, not a real per-year source. Both decisions are believed
   correct but flagged in case a human wants a second look.
2. **2023 and 2025 bundle poster/demo papers into the main DOI prefix**
   (unlike 2017–2022 and 2024, which keep them in a separate container/prefix).
   This doesn't block verification (DOI-prefix identity is still unambiguous),
   but it means a future harvester filtering "SIGCOMM full papers" by DOI
   prefix alone will silently pull in 36 posters/demos for 2023 and 14 for
   2025 unless it also filters by title pattern (`^Poster:`, `^Demo:`) or page
   length. Flagging for `pipeline/harvest_crossref.py` / aggregation-time
   attention, not something this report's scope fixes.
3. **`expected_per_year` proposed change**: registry had [50, 150] (a
   pre-data guess per the original notes, "Needs per-year Crossref data").
   Proposing [35, 110] based on the observed 2017–2025 range. Human should
   confirm this is the desired convention (raw DOI-matched count including
   bundled posters in 2023/2025, vs. a "full papers only" convention) before
   this number is used elsewhere.
4. **2019 stray DOI** `10.1145/3341302.3419024` is dated 2020 in Crossref
   metadata despite sharing the 2019 proceedings' DOI prefix — likely a
   late-registered/corrected DOI for a 2019 paper. Excluded from both year
   buckets rather than guessed into either; negligible (1 item) either way.

Otherwise: none.
