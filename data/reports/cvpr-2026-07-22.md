## Needs human review

- Only 2022 has a native OpenAlex source; all other years' works are orphaned (no `primary_location.source` link) despite existing in the OpenAlex corpus by DOI. This mirrors the ICRA pattern exactly. The recommendation is to use Crossref fallback with DOI-prefix validation for all non-2022 years, matching the ICRA approach.
- `expected_per_year` [1500, 3000] applies well to 2020–2025 (1482–2877 works/yr) but is too high for 2016–2019 (652–1309). The registry owner may want to widen the range or accept that older years will naturally underperform the current acceptance rate.
- DOI prefixes are NOT constant across years — each CVPR edition gets a unique numeric code in the DOI (e.g., cvpr42600 for 2020 vs cvpr52733 for 2024). Per-year DOI prefixes must be registered individually for Crossref harvesting.
