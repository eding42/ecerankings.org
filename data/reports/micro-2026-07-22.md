# Collection report: micro (architecture) — 2026-07-22

## Summary

Re-verified the existing Crossref year-by-year mapping for MICRO
(IEEE/ACM International Symposium on Microarchitecture) rather than
re-deriving it from scratch. Spot-checked 3 DOI prefixes (2016, 2020, 2023)
against live Crossref — all resolve and read as genuine MICRO proceedings —
then, since the check was cheap via `group_by`-style narrow queries, ran it
across all 10 registered years (2016–2025) for extra confidence. 8 of 10
years matched exactly; **2024 and 2025 had incorrect `doi_prefix` values**
(typos from the prior pass) even though their `count` fields were already
correct — corrected below. Checked for a 2026 edition: none found in
Crossref, and a web search confirms MICRO 2026 (59th edition) is scheduled
for Oct 31–Nov 4, 2026 in Athens — i.e. **not yet held**, so its absence is
expected, not a gap. Under the current "verified" definition (Crossref
coverage with DOI-validated counts is legitimate coverage; OpenAlex
conference-ingestion lag alone does not block verified), status is upgraded
from `candidate` to `verified`. Year window checked: 2017–2026 as instructed
(existing table already covers back to 2016).
