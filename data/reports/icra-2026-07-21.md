# Collection report: icra (robotics area) — 2026-07-21

## Summary

ICRA is confirmed as the flagship IEEE/IEEE-RAS venue, but it is **not**
fragmented into clean per-year OpenAlex sources the way ISSCC/DAC/IEDM are.
Only **2022** has a real per-year OpenAlex source (`S4363607759`, 944 works,
DOI-prefix cross-validated). Every other year in the 2016–2025 window exists
in OpenAlex only as orphaned work records (ingested, but `primary_location.source`
is null) — confirmed by direct DOI lookup — so coverage for those 9 years
relies on Crossref, DOI-prefix-validated (`10.1109/icra...`). A legacy
series source, `S4210217939`, is genuine ICRA but only covers 2007 (out of
window). One OpenAlex catch-all source, `S4306419799`, was found and
**rejected** as contaminated (mixes plausible ICRA-style titles with clearly
unrelated papers at very low per-year volume). Applied window: 2016–2025
(10-year default). 2026 was checked explicitly and found empty — see gaps
section. Overall confidence: high on identity and year coverage, status
`verified`.

**2026**: no genuine ICRA papers found. A Crossref search for 2026 initially
returned 55 "ICRA-looking" DOI hits, but every one of them belongs to a
distinct, unrelated conference — **ICRAI** ("International Conference on
Robotics and Automation in Industry", DOI stem `icrai70912.2026...`) — not
the flagship IEEE ICRA. A naive `doi.startswith("10.1109/icra")` check
would have silently included these; the corrected pattern (`icra` followed
by only digits/`.`, not letters) excludes them. Net: **zero** confirmed 2026
ICRA works in either OpenAlex or Crossref as of this report's date
(2026-07-21). Not included in `years{}`. Unclear from this data alone
whether that's ingestion lag or ICRA 2026 not yet having occurred/published
proceedings — worth a manual check if the orchestrator wants 2026 included
later.

**Orphaned-record pattern**: this matches PLAN.md's documented ~2yr OpenAlex
conference-ingestion lag, but here it's more severe — 9 of 10 in-window
years (all except 2022) are Crossref-only, not just the newest 1–2. This is
a wider gap than the IEDM/ISSCC/DAC pattern described in PLAN.md and worth
flagging to the orchestrator as an ICRA-specific data-quality note, not a
verification failure (Crossref coverage with DOI-prefix validation is
sufficient per the skill's "what verified means" reading).

orchestrator/user, not this worker.

  `openalex_ids`. Sampled titles are a mix of plausible ICRA-style papers

  Merging Aspects in Semantic Web", "Morals and 'AI in Health Insurance':

  container fuzzy-matched to the generic conference name, not a clean ICRA
